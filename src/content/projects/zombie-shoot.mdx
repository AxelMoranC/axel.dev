## The game

Zombies have overrun BunnyTown and the player has to save its residents. You move with W, A, S and D, aim with the mouse, and your weapon fires automatically toward the cursor. Zombies spawn in a ring around the player and close in, and every kill drops experience to collect.

It was built in a weekend at ConUHacks 2024, Concordia's 24-hour hackathon, by a team of four, and won the Beenox challenge.

## How it works

The whole game is about 320 lines of plain JavaScript drawing to an HTML canvas, with no engine or framework. A `requestAnimationFrame` loop moves the player, steers each zombie toward them, checks collisions with simple distance tests, and redraws everything every frame.

Difficulty ramps up over time. Zombie health, speed and spawn rate creep upward each frame until they hit fixed caps, so a run starts calm and gets frantic.

Progress unlocks new weapons:

- **Fire rate** increases every ten kills.
- **Bazooka**: a slow, large projectile on a three-second timer, unlocked by collecting experience.
- **Orbiting bullets** that circle the player, unlocked later in a run.

The map, the bunnies and the zombies are original pixel art drawn by the team.
